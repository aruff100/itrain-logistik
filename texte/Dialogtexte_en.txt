=== grundzuege ===
TITEL: Before you start: how iTrain-Logistik works
TEXT:
**iTrain-Logistik shows what is carried on your layout: passengers and goods that your trains take from station to station.**

**A file of its own, based on iTrain**
Logistik creates its own file (.logz). It contains all logistics details of your layout: towns, stations, industries and warehouses, the stocks and the details of your vehicles.
This file only works together with the iTrain file (.tcdz) it was created from. Track plan, blocks, stations and trains come from iTrain and form the basis. Logistik only reads the iTrain file, it never changes it.
Both must match. If a block is renamed in iTrain, a train composed differently or a wagon added, Logistik takes this over with the **Synchronise** button. If the iTrain file was saved since the last synchronisation, Logistik offers it right away when opening. The .tcdz the logistics file is linked with is always shown below the ribbon.

**What iTrain does not know**
iTrain knows length, speed and decoder of a vehicle, but not how many passengers a coach holds or how many tonnes of coal fit into a wagon. You add these details in Logistik:
- Wagons: wagon type, plus seats and standing places or load class and capacity
- Railcars and multiple units: seats or capacity directly at the locomotive

Logistik deliberately calculates with every single wagon and not with flat wagon lists. Flat lists would give far too simple traffic; only with the real wagons does it count whether a train really has room for the waiting passengers or the cargo ready for pickup. That is why Logistik reads all locomotives and wagons from iTrain when creating the file.

**The next steps**
- Right after creating, Logistik asks for the capacities of the wagons and railcars. Please complete them as fully as possible.
- Then set up stations, towns and industries under **Edit layout**, most easily with the traffic assistant.
- During operation iTrain reports which train stops where. Passengers then board and alight, and goods are loaded.

=== assistent_bahnhoefe ===
TITEL: Station setup
TEXT:
For each station from iTrain you set here what it is called, which type it has and what happens at its tracks.
- **Name**: how the station appears in Logistik (the name in iTrain stays).
- **Type**: station, halt, freight yard, fiddle yard or siding. As long as a station shows “Other”, you cannot continue.
- **Tracks**: **Platform** (passengers board and alight), **Loading track** (goods are loaded), **Platform and loading track** (both at the same track, e.g. in the fiddle yard) or **Nothing for now** (the track stays free and can be set up later).
- **On the layout or module**: if the station belongs to a module, choose it or create one with **New module…**.

If you choose platforms and loading tracks in a station, Logistik creates a passenger station and a docked freight yard.
You can call the assistant again at any time, for example to set up another track as a platform.


=== umgebung_personen ===
TITEL: Passenger traffic activated at “{bahnhof}”
TEXT:
Please add the surroundings the passengers come from and want to travel to.

- **Town**: many passengers, large demand for goods
- **Village**: about half as much as a town
- **Rural**: few passengers, demand mainly for food and farm supplies

Then the edit window opens with all details as usual. If the place already exists, choose **Choose existing place**.


=== umgebung_gueter ===
TITEL: Freight traffic activated at “{bahnhof}”
TEXT:
Please add what needs or produces goods at these loading tracks.

- **Town, village, rural**: need goods (food, wares, building materials …)
- **Industry**: produces goods and often needs goods itself (a manufacturing chain)
- **Warehouse**: takes in goods and passes them on gradually
- **Interchange**: goods come from outside the layout or go there

Then the edit window opens with all details as usual.


=== assistent_pruefung ===
TITEL: What should happen on the layout
TEXT:
The table shows who releases or needs how many passengers or goods per model hour, who the counterpart is and whether your trains can manage it.
- **✓** everything present
- **⚠** something is missing here, for example a supplier, a destination for the passengers or a train with suitable wagons

Select a row with **⚠** and choose **Next** (or double-click the row): the assistant adds what is missing there – an industry for a demand, more production at an existing industry, a fiddle yard if there is no counterpart, a warehouse for too much production. Then the table appears recalculated, with everything just added.


=== lieferkette_betrieb ===

TEXT:
To cover the demand of {menge} per model hour at “{ziel}”, an industry that produces it must be included: **{betrieb}**.

=== lieferkette_ort ===
TITEL: At which station is “{betrieb}” located?
TEXT:
The industry needs loading tracks so that trains can pick up or deliver its goods. Choose the station – any station of the layout works, only the fiddle yard does not (goods come from outside there, per track). If the station has no loading track yet, Logistik then asks which track becomes one. If the industry is not at any station of the layout, choose **Not on the layout**: it then always supplies, without a train.

=== transport ===
TITEL: What is transported from where to where?
TEXT:
Choose passengers or a good, the source, the destination and the amount per model hour. The assistant enters demand, production and the release of the loading tracks. What it cannot set itself, for example a missing recipe, it shows afterwards.


=== geraeusche_linux ===
TITEL: Sound cannot be played
TEXT:
The sound **{datei}** could not be played ({fehler}).

On Linux, iTrain-Logistik needs the system's ffmpeg libraries (libavcodec and libavformat) for mp3 files. They are often already present; otherwise install them with the package manager, for example:
- Debian, Ubuntu, Linux Mint: **sudo apt install ffmpeg**
- Fedora: **sudo dnf install ffmpeg-free**
- openSUSE: **sudo zypper install ffmpeg**

An audio output must also be present (ALSA, PulseAudio or PipeWire). Then restart iTrain-Logistik.

Files in wav format play without ffmpeg too. Sounds can be switched off under Logistics data → Sounds.
